FAQ

Where is my data stored?
Only on your device. We never receive or see it.

How do I cancel Radaa Plus?
Settings on your iPhone → your name → Subscriptions → Radaa.

How do I restore purchases?
In the app: Settings → Radaa Plus → Restore Purchases.

Is Radaa medical advice?
No. It is a logging and reminder tool only.
